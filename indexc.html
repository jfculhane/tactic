tactic-ux.com — Web Projects

Web design and development, shipped as working software.

I’m J.F. Culhane and this is a site for my consultancy that specializes in UX, Information Architecture, Strategy and IT Project Management. I have worked on the forefront of digital technology over the last 15+ years creating solutions and prototypes for digital content systems and social networks.

My domain concentrations are prototyping, digital content, and community development. I have worked with both B2B and B2C audiences and have managed large projects for Aon plc, Comcast, MSN, AOL, Scholastic, and PBS. I have also developed community, art gallery, and non-profit based projects that operate on a limited budget.

This site contains examples that I have used to better define projects in the past.

Innovative Firsts Ahead of the Curve: I worked with Newline Features on some of the first film trailers they put on the internet (1998), developed the first physicians’ channel for AOL (1998), helped create a neighborhood wireless network (1999), developed the first internet portal for all of Comcast’s Ad Sales (2005) and built the first statewide children’s environmental network website in the U.S. (2018).

Type
Embedded screening widget

Standalone HTML, WordPress / Avada embed

An interactive environmental health screener built for NYSCHECK, New York State's children's environmental health network. Nine branching questions route a respondent to one of five risk outcome categories, with regional routing built in so results point to the right local resources. Built to embed cleanly into a WordPress/Avada site and to hold up under small-group testing.
